How SatConnect Rescues Cutoff Villages

End-to-end fail-safe communication topology from isolated mountain valley to rescue headquarters.

1

Emergency Trigger

Villager presses SOS button on ESP32 beacon or web terminal. GPS fixes coordinates with 0% cellular network.

2

34-Byte Binary Pack

Incident is serialized into a 34-byte compact binary SBD frame with CRC16-CCITT checksum for high storm penetration.

3

Satellite Uplink

Uplinks to Iridium LEO constellation (780km) at 1621.25 MHz L-Band. Mesh crosslinks to ground station gateway.

4

Rescue Dispatch

SatConnect dispatch plots incident on OpenStreetMap, alerting NDRF battalions and local disaster volunteers.

Disaster Preparedness & Survival Manual

Offline guidelines for remote villages facing floods, earthquakes, cyclonic storms, and medical trauma.

Floods & Flash Surges

  • Disconnect main power & gas regulators before water rises.
  • Never walk through moving water; 15cm sweeps an adult.
  • Tie bright orange cloth on roof poles for air rescue pilots.

Earthquakes (Drop & Cover)

  • DROP, COVER under a sturdy table, and HOLD ON.
  • If trapped under rubble, tap metal pipes with stones.
  • Cover nose & mouth with cloth to avoid toxic dust.

Severe Hemorrhage / First Aid

  • Apply firm direct pressure with clean cloth over wound.
  • Apply windlass tourniquet 5-7cm above severe limb bleeding.
  • Snakebites: Immobilize limb below heart; DO NOT cut or suction.
